# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in
the PatchSentinel project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this community:

- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and gracefully
  accepting that your work may need revision;
- focusing on what is best for the project and for the people using it;
- assuming the other person is acting in good faith, and asking before
  concluding otherwise;
- accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from them.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- harassment, intimidation or discrimination in any form;
- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any
  kind;
- insults, put-downs, or derogatory comments, whether public or private;
- publishing others' private information - a physical or email address, for
  example - without their explicit permission;
- sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or other project activity;
- advocating for or encouraging any of the above.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces - the repository,
issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews - and also applies when an
individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at
`10754405+Herricka@users.noreply.github.com`. All reports are reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obliged to respect the
privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Enforcement actions, in increasing order of severity:

1. **Correction** - a private, written warning explaining the violation,
   with no consequences beyond that.
2. **Warning** - a warning with consequences for continued behaviour,
   including a period of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban** - from any sort of interaction or public communication
   with the project for a specified period.
4. **Permanent ban** - from any sort of public interaction with the project.
